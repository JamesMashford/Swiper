# Swiper

Two expectant parents swipe on baby names and find the names they both like. This glossary fixes the words used for names and for how each partner's swipes combine.

## Language

### Names

**Name**:
A given name for one gender. Charlie as a boy's name and Charlie as a girl's name are two separate names.
_Avoid_: Entry, record, variant

**Origin**:
The language or cultural heritage a name comes from, such as Italian or Scandinavian.
_Avoid_: Culture, ethnicity, nationality

**Market**:
A country or region (UK, US, Scandinavia, Italy, Brazil) whose public birth statistics say how popular a name is there.
_Avoid_: Country, region, locale

### People and space

**Nursery**:
The shared space of exactly two partners, with one surname and one set of filters.
_Avoid_: Couple, room, group

**Partner**:
One of the two people in a nursery. Each partner's swipes are private to them.
_Avoid_: User, parent, member

### Swiping

**Like**:
A partner's positive swipe on a name.
_Avoid_: Upvote, favourite

**Dislike**:
A partner's negative swipe on a name. It stays private and never hides the name from the other partner on its own.
_Avoid_: Reject, downvote

**Starter deck**:
The fixed, diverse set of names every partner sees first, before their own taste is known.
_Avoid_: Cold-start list, seed names

### Name states (per nursery)

**Unseen**:
A name neither partner has swiped.

**Pending**:
A name one partner has liked and the other has not yet swiped.
_Avoid_: Half match, one-sided like

**Match**:
A name both partners like.
_Avoid_: Mutual like, shared like

**Split**:
A name one partner likes and the other dislikes.
_Avoid_: Conflict, disagreement

**Rejected**:
A name both partners dislike.
_Avoid_: Dead, blocked

### Outcome

**Shortlist**:
The matches, ordered by each partner's ranking and head-to-head results into one combined order.
_Avoid_: Final list, favourites
